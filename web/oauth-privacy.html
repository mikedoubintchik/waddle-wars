Waddle Wars browser privacy

Publisher: Ninja Consulting LLC. Effective October 11, 2026. This notice covers the browser game and optional browser-account sign-in. The mobile launch privacy policy covers the separate free iOS and Android edition.

Optional account sign-in

You can begin core play without signing in. If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your name, profile picture, and email address with our authentication provider, Clerk, to create or access your Waddle Wars account. Clerk processes account and session identifiers, sign-in state, and security information needed to operate and protect sign-in. Google and Clerk apply their own privacy practices to their services.

Game and website data

When account-linked features are available and you use them, our Cloudflare-hosted service may store your account identifier with game saves, leaderboard scores, and a profile name you choose. Public leaderboard entries use your chosen game profile name or a generic penguin name, not your Google email address. The website and game service use Cloudflare for hosting, traffic delivery, and security; Cloudflare may process IP addresses and network details. The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for visit and performance measurements. When you click a Play in browser or Google Play link on our homepage, we count the destination and link location through Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine. This click count has no account ID, cookie, IP address, or message content.

The browser game sends a few automatic gameplay events to Cloudflare: opening the game, starting and ending a run, and a short category if a scene fails to load. Run-end events include distance, score, and fish collected. We use aggregate counts to find failures and improve play. We do not attach an account ID, name, email, token, save, or free-text error. The service uses the request IP only to limit abuse; it does not put the IP in the gameplay analytics dataset. Cloudflare Workers Logs records service requests and failures for operations and alerts; Cloudflare's standard retention and security practices apply.

Website and in-game feedback

The in-game Feedback form also includes the game version and platform to help reproduce issues. It sends only when you press Send Feedback, and does not attach a save, screenshot, account token, or purchase history.

If you submit the website feedback form, we email your selected category, message, and optional reply address to the publisher. Cloudflare handles the form request and limits repeated submissions; Amazon Simple Email Service sends the message, and Gmail receives it. Mail sent directly to support@waddlewars.io is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to the same Gmail inbox. The message is not posted publicly. Avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. Routine feedback correspondence is kept while we review or respond, then deleted from our active mailbox within 90 days after resolution, except for limited legal, fraud, or security needs. Provider backups may last longer.

Choices and requests

Sign-in is optional. You may avoid account-linked features and choose what information to send in a support message. For access, correction, or deletion requests about a browser account, email support@waddlewars.io. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. Do not email passwords, account tokens, or payment details.

We keep account-linked game records while needed to provide the account and resolve requests; specific deletion and provider-retention outcomes depend on the record and applicable obligations. Routine support email is kept while a request is open and deleted from our active mailbox within 90 days after resolution, except for limited legal, fraud, or security needs. Provider backups and security records may persist under provider practices; we cannot promise a fixed deletion date for those copies.

Sharing and security

We use Clerk for authentication, Cloudflare for hosting and game data, Amazon Simple Email Service for website feedback delivery, and Google when you choose Google sign-in or email us through Gmail. We do not sell your personal information. HTTPS protects service requests in transit, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact and updates

Questions: support@waddlewars.io. Material changes to browser-account features or data handling will be reflected here before those features are enabled.
