# Waddle Wars support retention procedure

Owner: the owner of Ninja Consulting LLC personally handles Waddle Wars support
and privacy requests at `ninjaconsultingllc@gmail.com`.

Operating procedure effective October 3, 2026, not a mailbox change or automation.
Gmail/Google processes messages under its own privacy policy. This procedure
controls the publisher's active mailbox; it promises no fixed deadline for
email-provider backup copies.

1. Identify only verified Waddle Wars cases. Check each message and attachment;
   a shared Gmail address, sender or broad search match is not sufficient.
   Keep unrelated Chancey and Ninja Consulting messages outside this procedure.
2. Retain routine correspondence while its issue is open. When resolved, record
   the resolution date and deletion deadline, no later than 90 days afterward.
   Record only the case reference and dates needed to track this deadline.
3. Review resolved Waddle cases monthly. Include deadlines falling before the
   next review, so deletion happens within 90 days rather than waiting until a
   deadline has passed. A reopened issue stays open until it is resolved again.
4. For a legal obligation or necessary fraud/security handling, document the
   reason, exact records needed and next review date. Retain only those records;
   remove the hold when its purpose ends. No blanket or undocumented holds.
5. Under the owner's control, verify the exact resolved Waddle messages and
   attachments, delete them securely from the active mailbox, and remove only
   those selected messages from Trash when appropriate. Do not empty shared
   Trash or purge unrelated Chancey/Ninja messages. Record completion without
   retaining copies of the deleted correspondence.
6. Handle privacy requests personally at the approved Gmail address. Explain
   applicable limits, including documented holds and provider backup practices;
   do not promise control over Google's separate processing or backup deletion.
