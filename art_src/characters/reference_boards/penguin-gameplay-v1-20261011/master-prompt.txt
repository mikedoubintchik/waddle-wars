Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: high-resolution character model and action reference board for Waddle Wars, a bright 3D mobile penguin runner game.
Primary request: Design one exceptionally appealing, buildable stylized juvenile emperor penguin and show THE EXACT SAME CHARACTER from multiple angles, running, jumping, and belly sliding. This is new art direction, not the ugly existing game mesh. Make the creature feel like a polished real-time 3D game character, not a plush toy or a flat illustration.
Composition: one wide landscape image, clean eight-panel board, 4 columns by 2 rows, generous separation. Top row labels left to right: "FRONT", "THREE-QUARTER", "SIDE", "BACK". Bottom row: "RUN", "JUMP", "BELLY SLIDE", "CHASE VIEW". One full-body penguin per panel, all extremities fully visible. Side view exact profile; front and back neutral standing. Three-quarter front neutral. RUN is a readable three-quarter waddle stride with one planted foot and counter-swinging flippers. JUMP is a three-quarter airborne arc, both feet visibly lifted/tucked, flippers opened for balance and separate ground shadow. BELLY SLIDE is an unambiguous side view of the penguin lying horizontal ON ITS BELLY on the ice, beak forward, flippers swept out/back and both feet trailing behind; never upright crouching, sitting, or lying on its back. CHASE VIEW is rear three-quarter from the actual slightly elevated following-gameplay camera; rear silhouette must be charming and readable.
Character design: compact rounded juvenile skull flows naturally into a pear-shaped upright body; subtle cheeks, short neck, fuller lower belly, broad but not spherical back, small tapered tail, elegant tapered flippers that attach anatomically. Head about one third total standing height. Soft charcoal and deep blue-black crown/back/flippers; warm ivory face and belly; restrained muted warm golden side-neck accent. Cute alert confident personality. Two expressive dark chocolate oval eyes set INTO sculpted eye sockets with soft integrated lids and restrained tiny white highlights; almost no visible sclera, no sticking-out eye balls/discs, no huge white goggles or rings. Eye shapes and size consistent across all panels. Small curved golden-ochre bill integrated into the face, softened upper ridge and subtle mouth line, not a flat duck disk. Small warm orange webbed feet with simple toe shapes and very subtle nails. No outfit or accessories.
Materials: believable clean game-ready satin/matte feather surface; broad attractive color masses, very fine subtle feather breakup only on close view, no coarse fur, etched waves, dirty noise, plastic gloss, chrome, white hot spots, glow, bloom, rim halos or sparkles attached to body. Restrained highlights retain dark form, body clearly three dimensional. Same face marking, colors, bill, flippers, feet and body proportions in every cell; same rig and anatomy, not eight alternative designs.
Scene: each cell uses a simplified pale blue ice ground and uncluttered soft winter-blue background, faint distant ocean/ice only, so silhouette reads. Consistent daylight from upper front-left and soft sky fill, grounded contact shadows and comparable exposure across all angles. In-game rendered aesthetics, crisp readable shapes at phone scale, not cinematic concept painting. Keep environment secondary.
Text: only the eight small clean labels specified above. No game HUD, captions, branding, watermarks, extra penguins, equipment or decorative props. High quality character art, coherent construction across views, at maximum useful reference-sheet resolution.
