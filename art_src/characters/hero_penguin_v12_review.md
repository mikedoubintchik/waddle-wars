## Correction and observed result

The first proof had broad torso facets, horizontal breast-like bands, a
protruding cheek plate, detached eye/lid/bill geometry, and sawtooth cream wing
inlays. Its albedo was also incorrectly used as bump height: low-contrast
8-bit pigment quantization produced false large-scale surface ridges.

The corrected source extracts the smooth implicit field directly through
marching tetrahedra with shared edge vertices. Cheek volumes now overlap the
head internally. Eye lens and lid vertices query the actual field depth;
bill roots are embedded and tapered. Cream body/wing regions use interpolated
paint weights. Albedo-derived bump is disconnected.

Rendered review confirms continuous torso shading, smoother bib, no separate
chin plate, and attached bill/eye roots. These are useful source corrections;
they do not establish reference-level fidelity.

## Remaining visible failures

1. Eye components face straight forward. True side still shows thin protruding
   lens/iris edges, whereas the reference has an eye seated around the curved
   head. Construct the complete eye in a local tangent frame oriented outward
   about the skull, including iris, pupil, catchlight, and upper/lower lids.
2. Cream hood boundary and warm cheek spots remain ragged because their paint
   masks are sampled as vertex colors. The warm patches appear as frontal
   blush instead of the reference's side markings. Author a continuous texture
   mask with controlled cheek/hood arcs and place warm markings farther back.
3. Surface remains smooth plastic. Feather normal/roughness response has not
   been integrated into this proof after removing invalid albedo bump. A
   correctly oriented, mip-stable normal field is required before material
   approval. Do not restore quantized albedo height.
4. Flippers have club-like rounded ends; reference paddles taper with a clearer
   leading/trailing edge. Feet read as overlapping pellets rather than fused
   webbed feet. Tail still uses stiff protruding wedges.
5. Face remains neutral and generic. Bill profile is now attached but lacks
   reference mouth-corner expression, nostrils, and upper/lower bill shaping.

Next meaningful shape change: tangent-framed eye assemblies and continuous
facial material masks, evaluated again from front, three-quarter, and true
side. Runtime integration remains pending until a convincing shape proof.
