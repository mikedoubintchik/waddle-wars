# Species selection portraits

New transparent bust illustrations for Adelie, Gentoo, King, Little Blue,
Rockhopper, Macaroni, and Chinstrap. Each was prompted as a single original
family-adventure-game penguin, head and upper chest, front three-quarter,
premium sculpted feathers, blue eyes, species-specific markings, no clothing,
cards, text, or environment. The approved Penguins mockup and Emperor portrait
establish the visual target. Runtime copies are 512×512 WebP.

These are static identity art. They do not depict purchased hats, neckwear,
eyewear, or trails. The live 3D try-on remains available separately.
