# Full-body Penguins menu species v1

Built-in ImageGen. Original Waddle Wars menu art. Source references:
`penguins_v1.png` (approved overall finish and body pose) and each matching
`*_portrait_v1.png` (species-specific face, bill, crest and color identity).
The Emperor base was extracted from `penguins_v1.png`, then its scarf removed.

Shared production prompt for the other seven species:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Waddle Wars Penguins menu full-body
> species cutout. Image 1 is the exact species face and material identity
> reference. Image 2 is the approved menu style and full-body scale/pose
> reference only. Generate one complete original penguin preserving Image 1's
> facial design, colors, proportions, eyes and bill. Rounded full body, two
> flippers and both orange feet visible, standing three-quarter front and
> smiling, with Image 2's premium animated-film 3D finish and soft sunset
> lighting. No scarf, hat, eyewear or other cosmetics. Centered character on
> genuinely transparent RGBA background with margin; no pedestal, scenery,
> interface, words, other penguins, halo or watermark.

Additional subject details specified per species: Adelie broad eye rings;
Gentoo sweeping white brows and longer bill; Chinstrap dark line under chin;
Little Blue blue crown and wings; King golden ear patches and upper chest;
Rockhopper wild yellow side crest; Macaroni long yellow eyebrow plumes.

Runtime WebP cutouts use quality 86 and alpha quality 95. Source PNGs remain
editable art references; runtime images collectively compress to about 1.8 MiB.
